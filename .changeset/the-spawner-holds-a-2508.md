**The spawner holds a NEW instance while the host's `MemAvailable` is below 5 GiB, and says so in the tick log (#2508).** On 2026-09-25 the host went from 25.7 GB free to 351 MB available while one runaway `node` ran, and a spawn in that window would have added a `claude` process to a host the kernel was already choosing victims on. `spawnWorker` now asks `spawnMemoryGate` (new `spawn-memory-floor.mjs`, a leaf) AFTER the role and BEFORE the claim's precheck and the claim, so a held spawn opens no pane and creates no worktree; a NEW `reviewer-<n>` is held too, before its checkout, while a live one is not asked because it adds no process. **A hold is a refusal, the seventh `no spawn:` line, and is never written to the ledger**: the order is offered again next tick and costs nothing toward `MAX_DELIVERIES`; a reviewer's cost is one tick of merge latency, weighed against a process added to a host whose kernel is choosing victims. **The floor is CHOSEN, not measured** (no distribution of `MemAvailable` over the tick ledger exists yet): above the runaway's 351 MB, well below the idle host's 23.8 GB, and not below M1's `MemoryMax` (4G) plus one instance (about 200 MB), which a test reads from `test-memory-cap.mjs` rather than restating. **An unreadable `/proc/meminfo` is neither a hold nor a silent pass**: the spawn goes ahead and the tick log says the reading was unavailable.
