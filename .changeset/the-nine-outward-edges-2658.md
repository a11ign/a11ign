The union is the product's four, and the tool's policy decides identically on a battery of inputs. **It changes no behaviour.** Two copies differ from their original by one named line beyond the header, because they name their own location. `agent-org-outward-edges.test.ts` walks the package for a boundary import and shows the walk sees one.
