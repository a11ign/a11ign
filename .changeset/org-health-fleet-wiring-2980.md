**The gate passes the fleet and waiting facts, so the idle-fleet signal can fire live (#2980, found by #2937).** `fleetIdleReading` existed and was tested, but `orgHealthNow` passed it neither `fleet` nor `waiting`, and `orgHealthReadings` reads an omitted `fleet` as "this caller does not ask" -- silent on purpose -- so the signal was dead and nothing logged a fault. `orgHealthNow` now passes `fleet` from `readFleetCaptures` (the ledger `fleet-watch.mjs` keeps since #2979, read here and not imported, because `agent-org-outward-edges.test.ts` forbids the tool importing `packages/control`) and `waiting` from `fleetBatchRows`' own selection of open `fleet-gated` rows. A missing or corrupt ledger and a refused open-rows read are `null`, an UNKNOWN on stderr, never idle. A ledger younger than 24 h with no capture is NO READING (silent), so its first day does not write an UNKNOWN every tick for `repeating-lines.mjs` to offer. **Known gap:** `waiting.labJobs` is always empty; the gate reads lab jobs that ended, none that are queued, so a fleet idle with only a lab job waiting does not trip. `orgHealthNow` is exported with an injectable clock, ledger reader and log so a test can run it.
