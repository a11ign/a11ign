**The gate now counts the tick's own repeating log lines and aged `backlog` rows, and offers each to the session that can fix it (#2848).** A line that repeats about a fault is a defect, and nothing counted them: the 7-day journal holds 944 `NOT RELEASED decline of N ... (Refusing rather than ignoring it ...)` and 935 `UNDELIVERED claim release not done` lines from a flag guard rejecting the gate's own flag, logged for days until the chairman's session fixed it by hand, because the tick is not a reader of its own stderr. `repeating-lines.mjs` reads `journalctl --user -u a11ign-work-tick` (one call, 0.1 s for 24 hours), splits it into COMPLETE ticks, normalises each line (timestamps, shas, digit runs) and, when the same normalised line is present in 30 consecutive ticks (about an hour, chosen from the 7-day table on the row), wakes `orchestrator` with the line, its count and its first-seen time. An allowlist of expected lines (`repeating-lines.allowlist.json`, every entry carrying its reason; the `SHELVED row` line is the first) is the only way a line stops being asked about. A second question in the same place wakes `product-manager` for a promotable `backlog` row older than 24 hours that carries no wait and no unpickable label. Two new JUDGMENT causes, `repeating-log-line` and `backlog-aged-unpromoted`; `readPromotableRows` now also asks for `title` and `createdAt` on the call it already makes.

**What it does not do:** rewrite any line it finds (each is its own row; #2853 is the first), run a model turn or a cron to read logs, or touch `wake.mjs`'s delivery path.
