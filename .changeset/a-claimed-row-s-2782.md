**Every removal of a worktree directory now writes a line, and a tree whose row still carries a `session:` label is refused by every remover (#2782).** `wt-2623` was deleted under its live claim twice and nothing recorded who did it. The prune's own journal could not say, because its `removed N worktree(s)` blocks interleave with the refusal sections; read by section, the prune (`a11ign-worktree-prune.service`) removed `/home/agent/repos/wt-2623` at 2026-09-28 04:08, 07:08, 08:08 and 17:08 (+01:00), each time a merged, clean, idle tree that #2020's `.a11y-owner` check let through, while the row was still claimed. `prune-worktrees.mjs`, `prune-tmp.mjs` and `row-claim.mjs` now append `removing` before and `removed`/`failed` after to `~/.cache/a11ign/worktree-removals` (path, branch, caller, reason, owner file), and refuse a removal whose line cannot be written. The prune and `prune-tmp` read the row first (`gh issue view`, only for a tree every other check passed), `decline` refuses a tree whose row carries another session's claim, and a row that cannot be read refuses rather than reading as unclaimed. The prune unit therefore declares the workers' `GH_CONFIG_DIR`.
