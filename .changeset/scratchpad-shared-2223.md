`.claude/rules/agent-practices.md` now tells every session two scratchpad habits: never capture output into a directory you are also reading (a `grep` over `tasks/*.output` whose own stdout landed there quoted itself to 3,688,951,808 bytes), and keep virtualenvs, wheels, weights and fetched corpora out of it, because every session shares it, it is counted in inodes as well as bytes and swept at one day, and a full one shows as empty output or ENOSPC rather than as a message about the disk. Three pins in `prefix-pins.mjs` are IMPERATIVE, so a copy in the destination cannot stand in, and `content-preservation.test.ts` asserts them with a control that fails when the sentence is deleted or only moved.
