**A declared wait names the CONDITION it waits for, and the tick re-reads it: a wait whose reason is gone is a stall, not proof of health (#2996, the chairman, 2026-10-02).** The freeze ended at 06:50Z and every wait it caused stood for four hours with `org-health` reading them as healthy, because `holdersOf` and `NOT_STARTABLE` treat a declared wait as proof of health. New leaf `wait-condition.mjs`: the closed grammar `Waiting-for: <closed|merged|labelled <label>|unlabelled <label>> <#n|owner/repo#n>` on a row or PR body (outside a fence), `conditionHolds`, and the set of wait-field kinds the gate reads (`WAIT_FIELDS`, with whether each clears itself). `pr:hold --until=<state> <ref>|manual` writes the reason as a marker comment (`holdReasonOf`/`holdExcused` beside `holdersOf`). The tick reads each referenced item (open lists first, then one bounded `gh api` read) and orders the SETTER to remove a wait whose condition is true; `ceo` is offered `stale-wait` after 30 minutes and `wait-without-reason` for a `hold:*`/`answer:*`/`blocked` with no readable condition quiet over 4 hours. `red-pr-unattended` and `no-merge-while-work-exists` no longer excuse a hold whose condition is resolved, a `manual` hold over 4 hours, or a reasonless hold quiet over 4 hours; an unread reference is excused, never released.

**What it does not do:** check that a ruling took effect, read `Waiting-for:` on `org-retro`/`queue-table` (which still ask the label), or order the setter through a cause of its own (it is the `org-health` cause, because `cause-declaration.mjs` is outside the row's Region).
